CheckMate Budget is a personal finance app built for people who are tired of wondering where their money went. Log expenses by hand, track every bill, set savings goals, and stay accountable to someone who cares.

Download for iPhone Free. Always.

What it does

  • Manual entry, on purpose

    Typing in an expense takes three seconds. That friction is the point — it keeps you aware of every dollar leaving your account, in a way that automatic syncing never does.

  • A real safe-to-spend number

    CheckMate calculates what you can actually spend right now — after bills, after expenses, after savings — so you always know where you stand without doing the math yourself.

  • Someone to answer to

    Pair with an accountability partner who gets notified about your savings goals and spending. Not to judge — to help you follow through when it's easier not to.

  • Bank balance, for reference

    Optionally connect your bank for a daily read-only balance and transaction view. It supplements your manual entries — it doesn't replace them.
